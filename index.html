11 / QUESTIONS

Before you
begin.

What do I need to begin? +

You need a laptop and a reliable internet connection. Some workflows can be done on a phone, but a laptop is highly recommended because it makes working with files, websites, research, and Codex much easier.

Is AAB for technical people? +

No. You do not need to be a programmer. AAB focuses on understanding what is possible, identifying the right work, and directing AI with enough context to produce a useful outcome.

Is AAB a self-paced course or tutoring? +

AAB is designed as a guided tutoring course. You are given a clear path through the ideas, examples, and workflows, so you can learn the thinking and apply it to your own work rather than just collect information.

Is this another prompt library? +

No. Prompts are one layer. The course is about the larger transition from prompt to workflow to agent to automation.

Does AI work completely on its own? +

Sometimes parts of a workflow can run with minimal intervention, but human approval remains important for decisions, payments, account access, publishing, and other sensitive steps.

What can I use AAB for? +

Research, content, design, websites, software, reports, customer workflows, personal organization, opportunity research, and many other repeatable processes.
